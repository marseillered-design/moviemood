How MovieMood works

Describe your mood in your own words

Type how you feel: “rainy Sunday”, “something light after work”, “dark sci-fi like Interstellar”. MovieMood reads the mood, genre, decade and favourite films in your words and picks well-loved titles to match.

Where can I watch it?

Every film and series page lists the streaming services, rentals and purchases in your country, with the trailer, the cast and similar titles.

Is it free?

Yes, and there is no sign-up. Favourites and watched titles stay in your browser, so watched films stop showing up in your suggestions.

Which languages?

English, Ukrainian, Russian, French, Spanish and German. The search understands queries in all six.
